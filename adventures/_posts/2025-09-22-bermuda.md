I just got back from a 5-day cruise to Bermuda with 20 family members and friends to celebrate my mom's milestone birthday.

On our final night, we had a party with a Honey Roast (re: [New Girl, season 3, episode 20](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Girl_season_3)). Unlike a traditional roast where the guest of honor is often hurled insults, in a honey roast they are showered with sweet memories.

Not a dry eye left the party.
